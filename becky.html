THE VAN

Becky's Ford Transit build.

A 2004 Ford Transit self-build camper that was never meant to be a £50,000 showroom van. Functional, over-engineered in places, unfinished in others, and constantly evolving.

Ford Transit Mk6DIY conversionOff-gridBuilt, not bought

The real-world build

Becky is the practical reference behind the site: the conversion, upgrades, repairs and the bits that absolutely did not go to plan. Full write-ups are added only when there is something useful to show.

The point isn't to pretend the build is perfect. It's to show what worked, what didn't and what we'd do differently next time.
