VAN BUILD TOOLKIT

Build the electrical system before buying the wrong bits.

Work out what you use, size the battery, plan solar charging and finish with the cable and protection for each circuit. The tools stay linked, so changing your daily energy use feeds the battery and solar estimates automatically.

Starting from scratch? Work through the tools in order. Already know what you need? Jump straight to any calculator. Example values are prefilled so you can see the tools working; replace them with your own.

1Power usageList your appliances and work out how much energy you use each day.2Battery sizeTurn your daily energy use into a realistic battery capacity.3Battery bankSee what series and parallel batteries give you.4Solar & MPPTEnter the numbers from your panel label and match an MPPT.5Solar yieldEstimate how much usable solar energy the roof may produce.6Cable & fuseEstimate cable size, voltage drop and circuit protection.
